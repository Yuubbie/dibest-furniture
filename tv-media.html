TV & Media Walls

The wall your living room is actually built around.

A media wall carries the whole room, so it has to earn its place. Built in solid wood with integrated lighting and brushed gold detailing, fitted to your TV size and the wall it will live on.

The Stone-Set Media Wall

A stacked stone backdrop with a backlit TV recess, flanked by walnut cabinetry with brushed gold pulls.

The Fireplace Media Wall

Built-in shelving either side of the screen with an integrated fireplace below, finished in dark oak with gold trim.

The Floating Console Wall

A slatted wood panel wall with a floating console beneath, lit from underneath for a lighter, more open feel.

Lit the way it should be, not as an afterthought

Warm, low-set lighting is built into the shelf lines and cabinet edges rather than bolted on later, so the wall still looks considered with the TV off.

Every media wall is measured against your actual television and console equipment before a single board is cut, so cable runs, vents, and access panels are planned in, not squeezed in.
