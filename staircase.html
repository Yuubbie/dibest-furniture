Staircase & Under-Stair Storage

The space under your stairs is worth more than a cupboard.

Built-in storage and display cabinetry fitted to the exact shape of your staircase, in wood finishes that match the treads rather than sitting apart from them.

The Storage Staircase

Drawers and open shelving built directly into the understair void, finished in the same wood as the treads above.

The Glass Display Stair

A tall glass-front cabinet built into the staircase wall, lit from within to hold books and display pieces.

Even the handrail gets the same finish

Brushed gold hardware carries through from the storage below the stairs to the handrail above it, so the whole staircase reads as one considered piece rather than a patchwork of updates.

Every layout is measured on site before anything is built, since no two staircases waste space in quite the same way.
