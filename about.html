Our Story

Built by hand, one piece at a time.

Better spaces start with patience

DiBest Furniture began in Abuja with a simple idea: that the pieces people live around every day deserve the same care as the rooms they sit in. Not furniture bought off a shelf, but furniture built for the exact wall, the exact light, the exact life it will belong to.

Every wardrobe, media wall, vanity, and cabinet that leaves our workshop passes through the hands of craftsmen who measure twice, sand by hand, and finish each joint the same way whether it will ever be seen or not. That standard does not change with the size of the order.

The philosophy

Better spaces, a lifestyle you deserve.

Furniture that fits the room, not the other way around

We start with a conversation, not a catalogue. Materials, proportions, and finishes are chosen for the space they will live in, so a DiBest piece never feels like it was slotted in after the fact.

Craft that does not cut corners

Brushed gold hardware, solid wood joinery, and finishes that are meant to be lived with for decades, not seasons. Quality that holds up under daily use is the whole point.

Leadership

The person behind the standard.

Patrick Chinagorom

Chief Executive Officer

Patrick founded DiBest Furniture on a simple belief: that dedication and resilience matter more than shortcuts. What began as a small operation grew, project by project, into a workshop trusted with some of Abuja's most considered homes and offices.

He still treats every commission the way he treated the first one: as a piece that has to earn its place in someone's home, built properly or not built at all.
